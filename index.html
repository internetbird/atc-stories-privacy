ATC Stories – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

ATC Stories ("the app") is an Android game that retells real aviation emergencies using public FAA radio recordings and NTSB reports. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

The app saves one setting on your device (whether sound is on or off). This stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Purchases

If the app offers optional story packs, purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. The app receives only a confirmation of which items you own, so that it can unlock them. It never sees your payment details. Google's handling of purchase information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
